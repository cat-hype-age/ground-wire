# Fiscal Year Conventions

- Pre-1976: U.S. fiscal year ends June 30
- Post-1976: U.S. fiscal year ends September 30
- Transition quarter (T.Q.): July-September 1976
- Calendar year always ends December 31
- When a question says "calendar year" or "as of December 31", do NOT use fiscal year data
- Calendar year-end data is often in JUNE bulletins of the following year
