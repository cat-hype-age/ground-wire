# Treasury Bulletin Terminology Evolution

## Reporting Convention Changes
- "Budget Expenditures" (1940s) → "Outlays" (modern)
- "Gross Debt" vs "Public Debt" vs "Total Federal Debt" — NOT interchangeable
- Pre-1976: Fiscal year ends June 30. Post-1976: September 30. Transition quarter (T.Q.) = Jul-Sep 1976.

## The Revision Pattern
- Data for month X appears in bulletins published AFTER month X
- Preliminary data marked (P) may be revised in bulletins 3-6 months later
- Revised data marked (R) supersedes preliminary
- October/September bulletins contain annual fiscal year summaries — richest data

## Common Table Naming
- FFO = Federal Fiscal Operations (receipts, outlays)
- PDO = Public Debt Operations (bills, notes, bonds)
- SB = Savings Bonds
- SN = Savings Notes (discontinued after 1970)
- IFS = International Financial Statistics
- OFS = Ownership of Federal Securities
- MQ = Market Quotations
- AY = Average Yields

## Footnote Conventions
- *, 1/, 2/ = see footnote below table
- "In millions of dollars" vs "In thousands" — ALWAYS check unit header
- "nan" in parsed text usually means empty cell, not "not a number"
- Dashes (-) mean zero or not applicable
